About Me

Hello! I am currently studying Web Development and Cybersecurity at Brookhaven College. This Beginning Web Programming class has been instrumental in building my foundation for front-end development, giving me hands-on experience building out functional, user-friendly interfaces.

Technlogies & Methods Learned

Throughout the semester, I've developed a variety of new skills:

  • Technologies: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and jQuery.
  • Techniques: Semantic markup, responsive design styling, DOM manipulation, and dynamic event handling.
  • Methods: Implementing complex form validation (utilized in my floral business project) and creating interactive data arrays (utilized in my shopping cart system).

Professional Goals

My ultimate goal is to bridge the gap between user-friendly front-end design and robust cybersecurity. By understanding the intricacies of JavaScript and DOM manipulation, I am better equipped to identify and mitigate front-end vulnerabilities like Cross-Site Scripting (XSS). Building these pages from scratch ensures I know how to secure web applications from the ground up.

Additionally, applying these technical skills alongside the leadership principles I've focused on through the NSLS will help me effectively lead a technical development or security team in the future.
